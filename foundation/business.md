# The business

High-level context about this business so the AI stops starting cold every session. Keep it short. This is the basics, not a full strategy document. Fill it once by running `setup.md`, then leave it unless something real changes.

Replace each line below with your own. Delete the examples.

## What we sell

> One line. The thing people pay for.
> Example: We sell a $49/month meal-planning app for people with food intolerances.

## Who it's for

> Broad strokes, not a full customer profile. Who is this for, roughly.
> Example: Home cooks managing a coeliac or low-FODMAP diet, mostly women 30-55.

## Where they find us

> The main channels. Where attention actually comes from.
> Example: Instagram, our email list, and Google search for recipe terms.

## How we sound

> A few words. The register, not a voice system.
> Example: Warm, plain, a bit funny. Like a friend who happens to be a dietitian.

## What we'd never say

> Hard no's. Claims you won't make, words you ban, lines you won't cross.
> Example: Never call it a "diet", never promise weight loss, never use "delve" or "game-changer".

---

That's enough for the AI to give you sharper output without you re-briefing it every time. The deeper work, your full customer profile, your real positioning, your voice tuned per platform, is what the content engine handles. This file just stops the goldfish problem.
